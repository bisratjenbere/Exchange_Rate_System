Who we are We support economic development of the country by financing projects.

 Development Bank of Ethiopia is established to support the socio-economic development of the country by availing medium and long-term credit to viable projects under-served by the financial system.

Priority Areas

Agriculture

The bank may finance projects engaged in irrigable mechanized commercial agricultural projects, modern livestock commercial breeding farm, and modern poultry commercial breeding farm.

Manufacturing

The bank may finance manufacturing industries engaged in processing raw materials, components, or parts in semi-finished or finished goods.

Mining

The bank may finance mining and extractive industries engaged in the extraction minerals, metals, and other raw materials or any other things that have economic values.

Ato Henock Tilahun 

Member since June 17, 2026

Currently, he serves as the Director of Operations at the National ID Program (NIDP) of Ethiopia, where he leads the nationwide rollout of the Fayda registration, the foundational digital identification system forming a core part of the nation’s Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI).

Prior to this role, he built his career at Hawassa University’s Institute of Technology, where he served as an academician and held institutional leadership positions, including Head of Department and Program Coordinator.

He holds a Master of Science degree (Cum Laude) in Geo-Information Science and Earth Observation, specializing in Water Resources and Environmental Management, from the University of Twente (ITC), Netherlands, and a Bachelor of Science in Irrigation and Water Resources Engineering from Hawassa University.
