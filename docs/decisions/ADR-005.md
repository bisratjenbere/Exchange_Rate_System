# ADR-005: Dashen transactional/cash identity proceeds as ambiguous

Date: 2026-10-10
Status: APPROVED (owner decision)

## Decision
Dashen transactional and cash observations will be stored with identity_status=ambiguous because no source-published date exists in those tables. These observations are excluded from the trusted current-rate view until identity is resolved.

## Reason
The LOCKED rule states fallback dates never participate in identity. The Dashen transactional and cash tables contain no explicit publication date. The owner confirmed to proceed with ambiguous identity rather than block adapter development.

## Consequence
Dashen transactional and cash rates will not appear in the trusted view until either (a) a source-published date is found on the page, or (b) the owner approves a documented source-specific fallback rule after verifying Dashen's publication behavior across multiple dates.
