- id is a currency row number, not a publication id. createdAt is identical across rows while updatedAt varies, so rows look edited in place and the API likely keeps no history.
- No field is shown to be an effective or publication date. updatedAt is a last-edit time; its meaning is unverified.
- The category of plain buying and selling is unresolved: for USD, EUR, and GBP they equal the transaction values. Do not assume cash.

- Weighted fields use both null (ETB, KWD) and "0.0000". Their difference is unverified.
- The ETB row (1.0000 everywhere) is the quote currency itself. Proposal: do not ingest as a rate.
